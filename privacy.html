Privacy Policy for Emoji Battery

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Emoji Battery (shown on Google Play as Emoji Battery: Customize Icon) is an Android app that shows a custom status bar with a cute emoji pet battery icon, themes, stickers and a just for fun battery troll. This page explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the app with package com.emojibattery.customize.icon, published on Google Play by Billionweb.

The short version

  • The app never reads what is on your screen. The Accessibility Service is used only to draw your custom status bar on top of the real one. It does not read your screen content, typed text, passwords, messages or notifications.
  • Your battery level, charging state, WiFi and mobile signal strength are read on your device only to draw the icons. None of it leaves your device.
  • The app checks only whether the camera or microphone is in use by any app (never what they capture), so it can hide the custom status bar and keep the system privacy indicator visible. This check stays on your device.
  • There is no account. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We use Google Firebase to count how the app is used and to receive crash reports, and Google AdMob to show ads in the free version. These receive device and usage information described below.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before ads are personalised or your data is used for advertising, and you can change that choice at any time.

How the app uses the Accessibility Service

Emoji Battery uses the Android Accessibility Service to draw your chosen emoji battery icon, stickers and status bar theme over the real status bar. Before you turn it on, the app explains how it is used and asks for your agreement.

  • It only checks where system windows are, so your custom status bar can hide while the notification shade or a fullscreen app is open.
  • It does not read your screen content, typed text, passwords, messages or notifications.
  • It does not perform actions for you, and it does not record or capture the screen.
  • No personal data is collected or shared through this service.

You can turn it off at any time in Android settings under Accessibility, Emoji Battery. Your custom status bar then stops appearing.

Information that stays on your device

To draw the icons, the app reads your battery level, charging state, WiFi signal strength, mobile signal strength and the time. It also checks whether the camera or microphone is currently in use, without any access to what they record, so the custom bar can step aside for the system privacy indicator. These are read live on your device and are never stored on a server, sent to us or shared with anyone. The design, theme, stickers, troll number and other settings you choose are stored only on your device.

Advertising and your consent choices

The free version shows ads. Premium shows none. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised and whether your data may be used for advertising and measurement. Until you choose, ads are not requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app's settings under Ad privacy. Elsewhere, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings under Privacy, Ads.

Subscriptions

Premium is a weekly subscription sold through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We receive no card details, no billing address and no name. You manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the Google services listed above, which act on our behalf or under their own terms as described. These providers may process information outside your country under their own safeguards.

Retention

Your settings remain on your device until you clear them or uninstall the app. Google Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months. Crash reports are retained by Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Advertising data follows Google's retention practices.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes everything the app stored on your device. To ask us to delete analytics data associated with your installation, or to exercise any other privacy right you have under local law, email arshpreet@billionweb.co with the subject "Emoji Battery data deletion". Because there is no account, we will explain what we can locate and what limited information, if any, we need. Never send passwords or identity documents.

Children

Emoji Battery is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use the protections these services provide.
